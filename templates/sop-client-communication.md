# Agency SOP: Client Communication
**Purpose:** consistent, predictable communication with every client.  **Owner:** Account manager.
## Channels
- Project updates, files, approvals: client portal / project tool (single source of truth)
- Quick questions: agreed chat channel; decisions get logged back to the portal
- Urgent issues: phone, then written follow-up
## Response times (business hours)
- Urgent (site down, campaign broken): within 2 hours
- Normal questions: within 1 business day
- Change requests: acknowledge in 1 business day, estimate in 3
*(adjust to your own SLA)*
## Cadence
- Weekly: short written status update (done / next / blocked / needs client)
- Monthly: report + review call
- Quarterly: strategy review
## Rules
1. Confirm every decision in writing.
2. Out-of-scope requests get a written estimate before work starts.
3. Bad news early, with a proposed fix.
4. Name an owner and a date for every action item.
5. Log holidays/out-of-office ahead of time with a backup contact.
## Escalation
Account manager → Project lead → Agency owner. Record the issue, resolution and follow-up.
