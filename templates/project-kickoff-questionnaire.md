# Project Kickoff Questionnaire
## Business
1. Describe your business and what makes it different in 2–3 sentences.
2. Who are your ideal customers?
3. Who are your main competitors? What do you like or dislike about them?
## Project
4. What problem should this project solve?
5. What does success look like in 3 and 12 months? Which metrics matter?
6. Must-haves vs nice-to-haves?
7. Hard deadlines or launch dates?
8. Budget range and any constraints?
## Brand & style
9. Three words that describe your brand.
10. Examples you like (and dislike), with reasons.
11. Existing guidelines, assets or copy we must use?
## Process
12. Who gives feedback and who has final approval?
13. Preferred channel and meeting frequency?
14. Anything that went wrong with past vendors we should avoid?
15. Anything else we should know?
