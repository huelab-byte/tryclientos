# Client Onboarding Checklist
## Before kickoff
- [ ] Contract / SOW signed; scope, deliverables, revision limits and timeline agreed
- [ ] First invoice or deposit sent and paid
- [ ] Main contact, decision-maker and billing contact recorded
- [ ] Client added to portal / project tool; welcome email sent
- [ ] Kickoff call scheduled; kickoff questionnaire sent
## Access & assets
- [ ] Brand assets (logo, fonts, colours, guidelines)
- [ ] Logins/access shared via password manager or invite (never plain email)
- [ ] Analytics, CMS, ad accounts, hosting, domain/DNS access as relevant
- [ ] Previous work, reports and research
## Kickoff
- [ ] Goals and success metrics confirmed
- [ ] Communication channel, response times and meeting cadence agreed
- [ ] Approval process and who signs off
- [ ] Project plan and first milestones shared
## First 30 days
- [ ] First deliverable / quick win delivered
- [ ] Check-in on satisfaction and process
- [ ] Recurring reporting set up
